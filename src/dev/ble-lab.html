BLE Lab — Virtual Shifting Hardware Harness

Throwaway research instrumentation for docs/virtual-shifting/experiments/. Two independent device slots (trainer, controller), each needs its own Connect button click (Web Bluetooth requires one user gesture per requestDevice()). Every log line is also mirrored to console.log as [BLE-LAB] <json> for tooling to read back.

Rider & Bike Settings

Feeds the Crr/Cw fields below (Send 0x11 panel) and is persisted as a cookie on this browser — see docs/virtual-shifting/experiments/10-offline-fit-physics-analysis.md for how these presets were derived. Rider/bike weight aren't wired into any calculation yet — the virtual-speed drivetrain model (VIRTUAL_SHIFTING_DESIGN.md §4.3) hasn't been implemented — they're stored here for when it is.
